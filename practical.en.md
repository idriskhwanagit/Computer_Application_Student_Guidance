# Mock and midterm guide — the hands-on questions

> **Every paper is hands-on:** the mock quiz, the quizzes, the midterm, the mock final and the final.
> In each question you do a task on your laptop, see what it shows, and choose the answer that
> matches it.

| Paper | Tasks | Marks | File |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mock quiz | 8 | Not counted | zip |
| Quiz 1 and Quiz 2 | 8 in each | 8, the average of both | Word |
| Midterm | 12 | 24 | zip |
| Mock final | 8 | Not counted | Word and PowerPoint |
| Final | 20 | 40 | Word and PowerPoint |

> ⚠️ **Every question comes in one of the ways below.** The answer is only in the file, and only
> shows once you do the task. Each group and each version has a file of its own: the same tasks,
> with other answers. So a friend's answer from another group or another version is no help to you.

> 💻 **On a laptop only** — they cannot be done on a phone.

> 🔓 **On an exam day only this guide stays open.** The lesson guide and the Questions and Answers
> tab are closed, so practise the basics and the ways at home beforehand.

## What you need

- **The laptop and its charger.** If your battery runs down fast, bring a power strip and sit near a
  socket.
- **The programs:** Chrome and WinRAR from lesson 4. For the quizzes and the final, Word and
  PowerPoint too.
- **The university account:** Chrome on your laptop must be signed in with your university e-mail,
  since the form takes university accounts only. If Google shows another account, use **Switch
  account**.
- **Your own internet** for the laptop, for example your phone's Hotspot, and enough credit. The
  phone is only for the internet, and stays in your bag.

## How the file reaches you

- **In the teacher's e-mail:** at the start of the paper the teacher sends an e-mail to your
  university Gmail, with the form's link and the file.
- **Before the exam day:** make sure you can sign in to the university Gmail on your laptop, and that
  WinRAR is installed.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| Google shows another account, or says you do not have access | Click **Switch account** and choose the university account. |
| The form says it is not accepting responses | The paper is not open yet, or its time is over. Tell the invigilator. |
| You cannot find the downloaded file | In Chrome press [[Ctrl+J]] → **Show in folder**. |
| The file's name has **(1)** in it | You downloaded it twice. It is the same file; use either. |
| **WinRAR** is not in the right-click menu | In Windows 11: **Show more options** → **WinRAR**. If it is still not there, WinRAR is not installed: tell the invigilator. |
| A yellow bar says **PROTECTED VIEW** | Click **Enable Editing**. |
| Something was changed by mistake | Delete the folder and **Extract to** the zip again; close the Word or PowerPoint file without saving and open it again. Then do the earlier tasks again. |

## The basics

- **Downloading an attachment:** in Gmail, move the mouse over the file and click the **Download**
  arrow. The file goes into **Downloads**, and in Chrome [[Ctrl+J]] shows it.
- **Opening a zip:** right-click → **WinRAR** → **Extract to** and the file's name. In Windows 11, if
  **WinRAR** is not in the menu, it is under **Show more options**.
- **Choosing several files:** keep your finger on [[Ctrl]] and click each one. For all of them,
  [[Ctrl+A]]: the bar at the bottom of the window says how many.
- **Copy, cut and paste:** [[Ctrl+C]] and [[Ctrl+X]], then [[Ctrl+V]] in the other folder.
- **Deleting and restoring:** [[Delete]], then in the **Recycle Bin** right-click → **Restore**.
- **Extensions:** **View** → **Show** → **File name extensions**. In Windows 10 it is on the **View** tab.
- **Word:** the font box, the size box and the **B**, **I** and **U** buttons on the **Home** tab;
  **View** → **Navigation Pane**; [[Ctrl+P]]; **Insert** → **Page Number**; **Layout** → **Margins**;
  [[Ctrl+A]] and the number of words in the bar at the bottom of Word.
- **PowerPoint:** [[F5]] from the start, [[Shift+F5]] from the chosen slide, [[Space]] to go on,
  [[Esc]] to end; **Animations** → **Fade**.

## The ways

For each way: what is asked, and what you do. The exam's questions are not word for word these, but
they come in these ways.

### Way 1 — Downloading and opening the file

**What is asked:** download the attached file and read its name, or open the zip and count the
folders inside it.

**What you do:**

- Download the file from Gmail and read its name with [[Ctrl+J]].
- In **Downloads**, right-click the zip → **WinRAR** → **Extract to**.
- Open the new folder and count the folders inside it.

> The mock quiz comes at the end of lesson 3, before WinRAR, so it uses **Extract All**, as its
> question says.

### Way 2 — Finding your way through folders

**What is asked:** a folder inside a folder, down to a file: the file's name, or a line inside it.

**What you do:**

- Double-click each folder until you reach the file.
- Double-click the file to open it, and read the line.

### Way 3 — Copying, cutting, deleting and counting

**What is asked:** copy or cut some files into another folder, or delete them and restore one, then
count how many files there are.

**What you do:**

- Choose the files with [[Ctrl]] and a click. Pictures and text files are told apart by their icons.
- [[Ctrl+C]] or [[Ctrl+X]], then [[Ctrl+V]] in the other folder.
- To restore: **Recycle Bin** → right-click the file → **Restore**.
- To count: [[Ctrl+A]], and read the number in the bar at the bottom of the window.

### Way 4 — The kind of a file

**What is asked:** the files of a folder look alike; count how many have a given extension, or give
a file's full name with its extension.

**What you do:**

- Turn on **View** → **Show** → **File name extensions**.
- Now you see every file's extension, for example **.jpg** and **.png**. Count the ones the
  question names.
- A Word file ends in **.docx**, and a PowerPoint file in **.pptx**.

### Way 5 — The browser and the laptop

**What is asked:** open an html page in the browser and read a word on it, or find, among a few
laptops, the one the lesson 1 table says is good.

**What you do:**

- Double-click the html file; it opens in Chrome.
- For the laptops: open each file and compare the processor, the RAM and the storage with the
  lesson 1 table. The RAM is on the **Installed RAM** line. In the final the laptops are listed in
  the Word file.

### Way 6 — Making the hidden visible in Word

**What is asked:** something in the Word file is hidden: a white word, a very small word, a line in
symbols, or a word under a picture. Make it visible and read it.

**What you do:**

- If a yellow bar says **PROTECTED VIEW**, click **Enable Editing**.
- Select the line and change what the question says: **Font Color** → **Black**, the font size, or
  the font to **Calibri**.
- For a picture: click it and drag it aside.

### Way 7 — Reading Word's settings

**What is asked:** click somewhere and say what Word shows: the font, the size, the **B**, **I** and
**U** buttons, the line spacing, the text direction, or the **Margins**.

**What you do:**

- Click inside the text the question names.
- On the **Home** tab look at the font and size boxes, and see which buttons are on.
- For line spacing: open **Line and Paragraph Spacing** and see which number has the tick.
- For the edges: **Layout** → **Margins**, and see which setting has the mark.

### Way 8 — Headings and the Navigation Pane

**What is asked:** which line is a real heading and which only looks like one; how many headings
there are; make a line **Heading 2**; or search for a word.

**What you do:**

- **View** → **Navigation Pane**. Only real headings show there.
- For **Heading 2**: click in the line, then choose it in **Styles**.
- To search: type the word in the search box at the top of the **Navigation Pane** and read the
  number of results.

### Way 9 — Pages and words

**What is asked:** how many pages or words the document has, or the number of the page that holds
something.

**What you do:**

- [[Ctrl+P]]: the preview says how many pages.
- [[Ctrl+A]]: the bar at the bottom of Word says how many words are selected. Hidden words count too.
- **Insert** → **Page Number** → **Bottom of Page**, then read the number at the bottom of that page.

### Way 10 — PowerPoint

**What is asked:** how many slides there are; how many you see with [[F5]]; which slide you are on
after so many presses of [[Space]]; an animation's number; or how many bullet points a slide has.

**What you do:**

- The slides are listed on the left. A hidden slide does not show with [[F5]].
- [[Shift+F5]] starts from the chosen slide. Each [[Space]] shows a new note or goes to the next
  slide. [[Esc]] takes you back.
- For an animation: select the picture → **Animations** → **Fade**, and read the number that appears
  beside it.

## The report — the final project

The report is not a question in a form: at home you make a report in Word and a presentation in
PowerPoint, and send them to the teacher by the university Gmail. To make the report well-ordered:

- **Length:** the report itself 4 to 6 pages, besides the cover and the table of contents, so 6 to 8
  pages in all.
- **Cover:** the report's title, your name, department, teacher's name and year, with no page number.
- **Sections:** **Layout** → **Breaks** → **Next Page**, after the cover and after the table of contents.
- **Page numbers:** in the report's own section turn off **Link to Previous**, then **Format Page
  Numbers** → **Start at** 1.
- **Table of contents:** headings as **Heading 1** and **Heading 2**, then **References** → **Table
  of Contents**, and at the end **Update Table**.
- **Captions for pictures and tables:** **References** → **Insert Caption**.
- **Sending:** save it as PDF and send it by the university Gmail.
